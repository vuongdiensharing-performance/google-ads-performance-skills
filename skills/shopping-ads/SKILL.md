# Shopping Ads

## Purpose
Diagnose Shopping performance across product data, structure, bidding, budget, and commercial economics.

## Use When
- Launching or auditing Shopping.
- Product-level performance needs investigation.

## Required Inputs
Product/feed data, campaign structure, spend, clicks, conversions/value, margins where available.

## Workflow
1. Validate product/feed eligibility and data quality.
2. Segment products by commercial value and performance.
3. Review campaign structure and bidding.
4. Identify wasted spend and scale candidates.
5. Check landing-page/product alignment.
6. Recommend prioritized changes.

## Output Contract
- Feed/data findings
- Product segmentation
- Campaign/bidding recommendations
- Commercial impact considerations
- Measurement plan

## Safety
Do not infer product profitability without margin or business-value evidence.
