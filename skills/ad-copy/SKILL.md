# Ad Copy

## Purpose
Produce evidence-led ad messaging aligned with search intent and landing-page value proposition.

## Use When
- Writing or refreshing Search ads.
- Creating test variants.
- Diagnosing weak relevance/message match.

## Required Inputs
- Keyword/theme and intent.
- Offer/value proposition.
- Landing-page content or summary.
- Brand/policy constraints.

## Workflow
1. Identify intent and promise.
2. Extract differentiators and proof.
3. Map message to landing-page experience.
4. Generate multiple distinct variants.
5. Check claims, policy risks, and redundancy.
6. Define test hypothesis and measurement.

## Output Contract
- Ad variants
- Intent/message mapping
- Differentiation rationale
- Test hypothesis
- Constraints/risks

## Safety
Do not invent product claims, prices, guarantees, certifications, or outcomes.
