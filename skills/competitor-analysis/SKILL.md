# Competitor Analysis
## Purpose
Turn observable competitor signals into strategic hypotheses without treating competitor data as complete market truth.
## Use When
- Evaluating competitor positioning.
- Planning competitor keyword coverage or differentiation.
- Interpreting auction/market signals.
## Required Inputs
Market, offer, geography, competitor set, and available search/auction/creative evidence.
## Workflow
1. Define competitor set and comparison dimensions.
2. Collect observable signals.
3. Separate facts from inference.
4. Identify positioning gaps and risks.
5. Translate into keyword, messaging, landing-page, or bidding hypotheses.
## Output Contract
Competitor map, evidence, strategic implications, hypotheses, risks, and test plan.
## Safety
Do not invent competitor spend, conversion rate, profitability, or internal strategy.
