# Audience Strategy

## Purpose
Use audience signals and targeting controls to improve relevance, discovery, and measurement without assuming audience membership equals intent.

## Use When
- Planning audience layering or observation.
- Reviewing audience performance.
- Building first-party or remarketing strategies.

## Required Inputs
Business objective, audience definitions, campaign type, available first-party data, and performance evidence.

## Workflow
1. Define audience role: targeting, observation, signal, or exclusion.
2. Assess intent and overlap.
3. Check eligibility/size constraints.
4. Evaluate performance and incremental evidence.
5. Recommend setup and test plan.

## Output Contract
- Audience map
- Role and scope
- Evidence
- Risks/overlap
- Recommended tests

## Safety
Do not assume demographic/audience correlation proves causal performance.
