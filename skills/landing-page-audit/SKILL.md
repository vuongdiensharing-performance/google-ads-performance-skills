# Landing Page Audit

## Purpose
Determine whether post-click experience supports the intent promised by the ad and converts qualified demand.

## Use When
- Search or campaign performance suggests post-click friction.
- Reviewing a landing page before launch or optimization.

## Required Inputs
Landing-page URL/content plus ad/keyword intent. Performance and conversion-quality data when available.

## Workflow
1. Map keyword → ad promise → page content.
2. Check relevance and clarity above the fold.
3. Review proof, trust, CTA, friction, mobile/usability signals.
4. Review tracking and conversion event readiness.
5. Prioritize fixes by likely business impact.

## Output Contract
- Message-match assessment
- UX/conversion findings
- Evidence
- Priority fixes
- Test ideas
- Tracking gaps

## Safety
Do not claim page-speed or technical metrics without measured evidence.
