# Performance Max Optimization

## Purpose
Evaluate PMax as a system while protecting conversion quality and avoiding unsupported conclusions from opaque reporting.

## Use When
- Auditing or optimizing PMax.
- Assessing asset groups, audience signals, budget, or conversion quality.

## Required Inputs
Campaign settings, conversion configuration, performance by available dimensions, asset/diagnostic data, and business outcomes.

## Workflow
1. Validate conversion goal and value quality.
2. Check campaign objective, budget, bidding, and location settings.
3. Review asset-group coverage and creative quality.
4. Review audience/search-category signals where available.
5. Diagnose incremental value versus cannibalization risk where evidence exists.
6. Recommend controlled changes.

## Output Contract
- PMax health summary
- Evidence and limitations
- Findings by component
- Prioritized actions
- Test/measurement plan

## Safety
Do not infer hidden query/placement detail that the available data cannot support.
