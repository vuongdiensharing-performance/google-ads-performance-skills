# Performance Max for B2B / Lead Generation

## Principle

PMax can support B2B lead generation, but account evaluation must focus on lead quality and business outcomes, not raw lead volume.

## Framework

1. Define a meaningful primary lead goal.
2. Ensure low-quality micro-conversions do not steer bidding unless intentionally used.
3. Connect platform conversions to CRM outcomes where possible.
4. Monitor qualified-lead rate, opportunity rate, and revenue quality.
5. Evaluate conversion lag before changing targets.

## Anti-patterns

- Optimizing toward page views, button clicks, or unqualified submissions as if they were revenue.
- Scaling because lead volume increased while qualified lead rate fell.
