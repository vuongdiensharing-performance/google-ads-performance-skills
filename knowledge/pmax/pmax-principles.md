# Performance Max Principles

## Principle

Performance Max uses Google AI across bidding, targeting, creative, and attribution. Diagnose it using the campaign's business goal and the quality of conversion/value signals rather than treating it like a Search campaign.

## Evaluation framework

- Confirm the primary conversion goal is correct.
- Review conversion lag before judging recent performance.
- Review campaign-level results plus available insights, search impact, audience trends, creative evaluation, and channel-level reporting.
- Separate measurement problems from delivery problems.
- Avoid frequent major changes during the learning period.

## Controls

Use exclusions and controls deliberately. Negative keywords can be highly restrictive in PMax; use them for essential brand-safety or completely irrelevant queries and prefer the appropriate brand-exclusion mechanism for brand protection where applicable.
