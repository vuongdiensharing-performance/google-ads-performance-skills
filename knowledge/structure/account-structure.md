# Account Structure

## Principle

Structure campaigns around materially different business intent, budget, geography, product, or measurement requirements. Avoid fragmentation that prevents campaigns from receiving useful data.

## Core pattern

- Brand Search
- Non-brand Search by meaningful intent/theme
- Competitor Search when strategically justified
- Performance Max or Shopping as separate campaign types
- Remarketing / audience-specific campaigns only when a distinct objective or control is required

## Decision guidance

- Separate campaigns when budget, bidding, geography, product, or reporting needs differ materially.
- Prefer thematic ad groups over unnecessary one-keyword fragmentation.
- Keep naming and hierarchy consistent enough for reporting and automation.
- Review overlap and cannibalization before creating new campaigns.

## Anti-patterns

- Splitting campaigns solely to make the account look organized.
- Creating many low-volume ad groups with no distinct intent.
- Mixing materially different economics under one budget without a reason.
