# Search Term Methodology

## Principle

Search terms are the strongest direct evidence of what users actually searched before an ad was shown. Use them to refine targeting and discover valuable demand.

## Workflow

1. Set an appropriate lookback window.
2. Segment by campaign, ad group, keyword, intent, spend, and outcome.
3. Identify irrelevant or harmful query patterns.
4. Identify high-value queries that deserve keyword expansion.
5. Check whether a proposed negative would block valuable variants.
6. Route decisions to negative-keyword, keyword-research, or campaign-structure workflows.

## Evidence

Prefer combinations of spend, clicks, conversions, conversion value, and business-quality outcomes. Do not mark a term negative solely because it has low volume.
