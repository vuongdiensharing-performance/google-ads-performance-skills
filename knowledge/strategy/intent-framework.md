# Intent Framework

## Principle

Google Ads strategy should prioritize capturing existing demand before expanding into weaker or more speculative demand.

## Intent ladder

1. Brand intent
2. High-intent non-brand
3. Competitor intent
4. Problem-aware intent
5. Demand generation / awareness

## Decision guidance

- Prove the strongest intent tier before materially expanding into the next tier.
- Separate brand demand from non-brand demand when budget or reporting clarity requires it.
- Evaluate intent using business outcomes, not CTR alone.
- Use search terms and downstream conversion quality to validate whether a keyword actually represents valuable demand.

## Exceptions

A lower-intent tier may be justified when search volume is insufficient, the business objective is explicitly demand generation, or upper-funnel economics are measured separately.

## Anti-patterns

- Scaling broad awareness while high-intent demand is unprofitable or unmeasured.
- Treating all conversions as equally valuable.
- Using CTR as a proxy for purchase intent.
