# Funnel Strategy

## Principle

Google Ads optimization should be evaluated against the business funnel, not only platform-level media metrics.

## Funnel model

Impression → Click → Landing Page Visit → Conversion → Qualified Conversion → Opportunity → Revenue.

## Decision guidance

- Define the primary business outcome before selecting campaign and bidding objectives.
- Treat platform conversions as signals whose quality must be validated downstream.
- Diagnose leakage by stage before changing media settings.
- Separate acquisition efficiency from lead or revenue quality.
- When downstream data is unavailable, state the measurement gap instead of assuming conversion quality.

## Anti-patterns

- Optimizing a low-quality conversion event because it is plentiful.
- Comparing campaigns with different conversion definitions as if they were equivalent.
- Using CTR or CPC as the final business KPI.
