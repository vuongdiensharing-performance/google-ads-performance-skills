# Conversion Framework

## Principle

Optimization is only as reliable as the conversion signals supplied to the bidding system and used in reporting.

## Primary vs secondary

Primary conversion actions are eligible for bidding optimization and are included in the standard Conversions column when their goal is used for bidding. Secondary actions are generally observation-only and appear in All conversions.

## Decision framework

- Define the business outcome first.
- Map the outcome to a conversion action.
- Decide which actions are primary versus secondary.
- Validate tracking, deduplication, attribution, value, and data freshness.
- Compare platform conversions with CRM or revenue outcomes where available.

## Lead-generation rule

A form submission should not automatically be treated as a high-quality business outcome. Where CRM stages exist, evaluate lead quality and qualified-lead rate separately.
