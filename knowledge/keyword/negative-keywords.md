# Negative Keywords

## Principle

Negative keywords are a control mechanism for preventing materially irrelevant or commercially harmful query traffic. They should be selected from evidence, not from assumptions about individual words.

## Decision guidance

- Review the full query meaning and intent before excluding a term.
- Prefer recurring irrelevant patterns when they can be safely excluded.
- Distinguish campaign-level exclusions from ad-group-level exclusions according to the desired scope.
- Check for valuable variants before applying broad exclusions.
- Use downstream conversion quality when available to distinguish cheap but weak traffic from genuinely valuable demand.

## Evidence

Useful evidence includes query text, spend, clicks, conversions, conversion value, lead quality, and recurrence.

## Anti-patterns

- Negating every term with zero conversions regardless of spend or volume.
- Negating a broad word because one query containing it was poor.
- Using negatives to compensate for incorrect campaign structure.
