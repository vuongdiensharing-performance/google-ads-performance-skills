# Keyword Intent

## Principle

Classify keywords by the user's likely commercial intent before deciding campaign, ad-group, match-type, and budget treatment.

## Intent classes

- Navigational / brand
- Commercial investigation
- Transactional / high intent
- Problem-aware
- Informational / low commercial intent
- Competitor

## Decision guidance

- Prefer explicit commercial intent for direct-response campaigns.
- Do not infer quality from keyword wording alone; validate with search terms and downstream outcomes.
- Map distinct intent themes to coherent ad messaging and landing pages.
- Preserve enough query volume for meaningful optimization.

## Anti-patterns

- Grouping unrelated intent under one ad group.
- Treating every keyword with a high CTR as commercially valuable.
- Expanding keyword volume without checking query quality.
