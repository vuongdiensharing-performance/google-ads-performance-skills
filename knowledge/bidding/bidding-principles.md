# Bidding Principles

## Core principle

Choose bidding based on the business outcome being optimized, the quality of conversion signals, available evidence, and the campaign's constraints.

## Smart Bidding

Google defines Smart Bidding as auction-time optimization toward conversions or conversion value. Current named strategies include Maximize conversions, Target CPA, Maximize conversion value, and Target ROAS.

## Decision framework

1. Validate conversion tracking and primary conversion goals.
2. Identify the business objective: volume, efficiency, value, or return.
3. Assess conversion volume, value stability, and conversion lag.
4. Select a strategy compatible with the objective.
5. Set targets from credible historical economics rather than arbitrary aspirations.
6. Allow sufficient learning time before making repeated changes.

## Guardrails

- Do not diagnose bidding from CPA/ROAS alone.
- Do not change bidding targets repeatedly during a learning period without evidence.
- A bidding problem may actually be a conversion-quality or measurement problem.
