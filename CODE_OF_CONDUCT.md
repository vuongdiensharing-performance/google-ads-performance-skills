# Code of Conduct

We want this project to remain a professional, evidence-driven, and welcoming technical community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss evidence, implementation, and trade-offs in good faith.
- Give credit to sources and contributors.
- Protect confidential information.
- Report security concerns privately rather than exploiting them.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate submission of malicious code, Skills, Rules, fixtures, or dependencies.
- Publishing credentials, customer data, or private security reports.
- Attempts to bypass review, validation, safety controls, or Human Approval requirements.

Maintainers may remove content or contributions that violate these standards or materially threaten the integrity or safety of the project.
